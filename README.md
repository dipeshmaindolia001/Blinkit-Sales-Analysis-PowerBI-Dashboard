# Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Power BI dashboard built using Blinkit's sales dataset. The objective of the project is to analyze sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution to uncover business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard transforms raw data into interactive visualizations, helping stakeholders identify trends, evaluate outlet performance, and optimize business strategies.

---

## Business Requirement

To conduct a comprehensive analysis of Blinkit's sales performance, customer satisfaction, and inventory distribution to identify key insights and opportunities for optimization using Power BI.

---

## KPIs Tracked

- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Number of Items Sold
- Average Rating

---

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Total Sales by Fat Content
**Objective:** Analyze the impact of fat content on total sales.

**Additional Insights:**
- Compare Average Sales across fat categories.
- Compare Number of Items sold.
- Compare Average Ratings.

**Visualization Used:** Donut Chart

---

### 2. Total Sales by Item Type
**Objective:** Identify which item categories contribute the most to overall sales.

**Additional Insights:**
- Compare Average Sales.
- Compare Number of Items.
- Compare Average Ratings.

**Visualization Used:** Bar Chart

---

### 3. Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales
**Objective:** Compare sales performance across outlets segmented by fat content.

**Additional Insights:**
- Evaluate differences in Average Sales.
- Compare Number of Items sold.
- Compare Average Ratings.

**Visualization Used:** Stacked Column Chart

---

### 4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment
**Objective:** Evaluate how the age of outlet establishments influences total sales.

**Visualization Used:** Line Chart

---

### 5. Sales by Outlet Size
**Objective:** Analyze the relationship between outlet size and total sales.

**Visualization Used:** Donut/Pie Chart

---

### 6. Sales by Outlet Location
**Objective:** Assess the geographic distribution of sales across different outlet locations.

**Visualization Used:** Funnel Map

---

### 7. All Metrics by Outlet Type
**Objective:** Provide a complete view of outlet performance across key business metrics.

**Metrics Included:**
- Total Sales
- Average Sales
- Number of Items
- Average Rating

**Visualization Used:** Matrix Card
